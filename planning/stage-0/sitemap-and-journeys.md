# Sitemap and visitor journeys

## Primary navigation

| Label   | Route    | Purpose                                                                                |
| ------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RAY SHA | `/`      | Mixed editorial opening with immediate representative work                             |
| INDEX   | `/index` | The full archive in one browseable collection without institutional categories         |
| WORK    | `/work`  | Substantial features, collections, reports, and visual bodies of work                  |
| NOTES   | `/notes` | Observations, fragments, failed attempts, small discoveries, and unresolved questions  |
| ABOUT   | `/about` | Short first-person introduction, selected factual links, documents, and contact method |

`ARCHIVE` is reserved as a possible future label and is not part of the initial navigation.

## Detail routes

- `/work/[slug]` — substantial feature or collection.
- `/notes/[slug]` — individually shareable note when a note needs its own page.
- `/documents/[slug]` — metadata page for a downloadable or viewable document; files themselves live under versioned media paths.
- `/objects/[slug]` is reserved. Objects should normally appear inside features, collections, or the index unless they need a durable standalone record.

## Homepage sequence

The opening line is `Work, questions, pictures, arguments, and a few unfinished things.` It is followed immediately by a deliberately uneven sequence:

1. Kirigami environmental robot — large visual opening; making and testing.
2. Pesticide field research — serious research feature with one concrete measurement or field detail.
3. Chinese painting — image-led pause with minimal captioning.
4. Said Out Loud — a brief speech, debate, or hosting fragment centered on a specific moment.
5. Water seen from above — drone and water-quality investigation.
6. Cycling note — short ordinary-life observation; deliberately not inflated.
7. Environmental discounting figure — working-paper fragment with uncertainty stated.
8. Raspberry Pi photography — compact object story about building an unnecessarily complicated camera.

This is a compositional plan, not permission to fabricate missing copy or media. Entries without cleared evidence remain private until ready.

## Primary visitor journey

1. Arrive and see work immediately.
2. Recognize that unlike things coexist rather than sit in separate resume categories.
3. Open one substantial feature.
4. Follow a quiet tag or related-entry link into a different medium.
5. Visit the index for breadth or About for concise factual context.

## Secondary journeys

- **Research reader:** pesticide feature → source document/figure → sensing note → climate-economics working paper.
- **Making reader:** kirigami feature → failed version/object → environmental sensing → Raspberry Pi camera.
- **Visual reader:** painting → photography collection → drone image → cycling note.
- **Speaking/leadership reader:** Said Out Loud → ASB systems story → MUN/debate note → environmental collaboration.
- **Returning reader:** Notes → recent entries → related older work through shared tags.

## Connections reserved for Stage 5

The content model records relationships now so the later interaction does not require reauthoring. Initial relationship candidates include:

- kirigami robot ↔ environmental sensing — a mechanism became a way to collect data;
- pesticide research ↔ climate economics — measurement and the decisions that follow it;
- Chinese painting ↔ photography — framing, restraint, and attention;
- drone water inspection ↔ cycling — movement changes what becomes visible;
- debate ↔ research notes — claims, evidence, and revision;
- erhu ↔ plotted data — lines that change meaning through small variation.

These links are editorial hypotheses and should be confirmed against real material before publication.
